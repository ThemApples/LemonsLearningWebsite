Welcome

Overview

This article provides step-by-step instructions on how to navigate and utilize our ServiceNow look-alike technical documentation portal to remove environment roadblocks. From absolute initial local configuration sequences to continuous downstream cloud integration pipelines, this centralized knowledge base is required for all developers, engineers, and IT staff seeking to turn architectural legacy lemons into seamless, scalable lemonade.

Prerequisites

  • An internet connection
  • This pages URL
  • Your mobile device configured with the Authenticator app for MFA.

How to use

  1. Locate the Navigation Bar: Look at the primary toolbar running horizontally across the top of your portal interface.
  2. Find the Knowledge Base Icon or Link: Look for a button explicitly labeled "Knowledge Base" and pressed on it
  3. Click the Button: Click directly on the item to open the central repository for technical documentation, local setup guides, and troubleshooting runbooks.
