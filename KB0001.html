Enterprise Security

Overview

This guide details the essential security standards and best practices requires across an enterprise environment

Prerequisites

  • Multifactor Authenication
  • A Strong password
  • An Email

How to use

  1. Mulifactor Authenication: Download **[Approved Authenticator App]** from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store on your mobile device.(If you are not sure what is authenicator app is recommended please get in contact your helpdesk team)
  2. Create a Strong Password: Your password must be at least 8 characters long and include at least one uppercase letter (A–Z), one lowercase letter (a–z), one number (0–9) and one special character(such as `!`, `@`, `#`, `$`, `%`, `&`, or `*`). Reminder, Please update the password every three months.
  3. An Email: If you notice suspicious login alerts, unauthorized password changes, or unusual account activity, contact the IT Service Desk immediately. Do not attempt to fix or investigate the issue on your own—reporting it quickly helps our security team contain potential threats. (Please provide this information to the service desk team, How did you discover you were hacked? and Do you have any sensitive information on your device?)
