How to Connect to Global VPN Securely

Overview

This article provides step-by-step instructions on how to connect to the corporate Global VPN network securely using your multi-factor authentication (MFA) credentials. This is required for all remote employees and contractors accessing internal resources.

Prerequisites

  • An active corporate network account.
  • Global Protect VPN Client installed on your machine.
  • Your mobile device configured with the Authenticator app for MFA.

Connection Steps

  1. Open the Global Protect Client from your system tray (Windows) or menu bar (macOS).
  2. Enter the portal address: vpn.enterprise-secure.com and click Connect.
  3. When prompted, input your standard corporate username and password.
  4. Complete the MFA prompt sent to your mobile authenticator app.
Note: If you experience authentication lockouts after three incorrect attempts, please contact the IT Service Desk.

Troubleshooting

If you encounter error code Connection Timed Out, verify that your local internet connection is working and that you are not running a conflicting third-party personal VPN service.
